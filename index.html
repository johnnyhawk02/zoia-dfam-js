About this port

The DSP runs per sample in an AudioWorklet and follows the patch's 132 modules and 169 cables: two oscillators with triangle/square switches, pitch envelopes scaled by velocity, linear FM from VCO 1 into VCO 2, a bit-XOR "sync", white noise, a state-variable filter with envelope and noise modulation, and a VCA. Knob defaults are the values saved in the patch.

Pitch follows ZOIA's scale: 0 is 27.5 Hz and each 0.1 of CV is one octave, so a centred Frequency knob sits at 27.5 Hz. The Breaks row is the patch's 16-step restart sequencer; Modifiers 1–4 light steps 6, 10, 4 and 13. MIDI clock, MIDI note triggering, the external CV jack and the display pixels are not ported.

Audio is off. Press Start audio, then Run.
